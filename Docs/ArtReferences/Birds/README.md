# BomberBird Bird Concept References

These images preserve the approved visual direction for the current bird cast. They are concept references, not production-ready sprite sheets. Individual sprites, transparent backgrounds, animation frames, pivots, and Unity import settings still need to be created.

## Approved Player Birds

| Bird | Reference | Key visual traits |
|---|---|---|
| Eurasian hoopoe | [Concept sheet](hoopoe-approved-concept.png) | Orange fan crest with black tips, striped wings, long dark bill |
| Great white pelican | [Concept sheet](great-white-pelican-approved-concept.png) | Cream-white rounded body, oversized golden bill and pouch, small orange feet |
| Chukar partridge | [Concept sheet](chukar-partridge-approved-concept.png) | Warm gray-tan body, cream throat, dark necklace, striped flanks, coral feet |
| White-throated kingfisher | [Concept sheet](white-throated-kingfisher-approved-concept.png) | Chestnut head and body, white throat, turquoise wings, coral-red bill |

## Approved Enemy Bird

| Bird | Reference | Key visual traits |
|---|---|---|
| Common myna | [Concept sheet](common-myna-approved-concept.png) | Brown body, charcoal head, yellow eye patches, bill, and feet |

The current myna sheet represents the regular enemy. A much larger boss myna with smaller myna followers has been discussed, but its design and gameplay are still open.

## Art Direction

- Original retro 16-bit-inspired pixel art.
- Compact, readable silhouettes suitable for a top-down grid arena.
- Friendly player characters and a mischievous, readable enemy.
- The round golden seed pod with a green leaf is the approved pod direction shown in these sheets.

The concept images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool during the BomberBird design session. They were approved as visual references and saved to the repository on 2026-09-08.
